Rent Ledger for Small Landlords: Partial Payments + Late Fees (Excel & Google Sheets)

A rent ledger for landlords with 1-10 units who have outgrown a basic income log.

What it does:

  • Matches every payment to a specific charge, so you see what is still owed on each month's rent
  • Handles partial payments, with a payment method and a note on each one
  • Calculates late fees from a grace period and a daily rate that you set for each tenant, with an optional cap
  • Shows balance, late fees outstanding and total due per tenant on a summary sheet
  • Includes a proration helper for mid-month move-ins
  • Flags typing mistakes (misspelled tenant, unknown charge ID, overpayment)

Works in Excel 2016+ and Google Sheets. No macros, no add-ins, no accounts. Comes with made-up sample data and a step-by-step README.

How it works and its limits:

  • You type each charge yourself; charges are not created automatically.
  • A payment that covers several charges must be split into one row per charge.
  • Payments for a charge must be entered oldest first.
  • Late fee = days late x daily rate, optionally capped. Days are counted to the date the charge was fully paid, or to the report date. No compounding and no per-dollar fees.
  • Capacity: 10 tenants, 299 charge rows, 599 payment rows.
  • Proration uses one common days-remaining method only.

Not included: automatic monthly charges, expense, deposit, tax or reserve tracking, reminders, or invoices.

Late fee, grace period and proration rules differ by place. All of them are settings you fill in, and you must check your local rules and your lease yourself. This is a record-keeping tool, not legal, tax or financial advice.
