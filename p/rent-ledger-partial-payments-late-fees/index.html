A complete rent ledger for landlords with 1 to 10 units. Fill in your tenants once. After that, the only regular work is typing each payment as it arrives: date, tenant, amount.

What it does for you:

  • Charges the rent every month from each tenant's rent, due day and lease dates. Type over any month for a part month, an increase or a free month.
  • Applies every payment to that tenant's oldest unpaid charge, so part payments and one payment for two months need nothing special.
  • Works out late fees from your own lease terms for each tenant: grace days, a flat fee, a percentage, a fee per day late, and a limit.
  • Dashboard: total due, unpaid rent, unpaid late fees, overdue charges, and one coloured line per tenant.
  • Year view: every tenant's twelve months in colour, plus money received each month for tax time.
  • Printable statement for any tenant: every charge, payment, late fee and the total due.
  • Printable receipt for any payment.
  • Part-month rent helper, deposits held, other charges (repairs, utilities, fees).
  • Drop-down lists and a Check column that flag typing mistakes.

Works in Excel 2016+ and Google Sheets. The sample file was checked in both. No macros, no add-ins, no accounts. Comes with made-up sample tenants so you can see it working the moment you open it, plus a step-by-step README.

Late fee and grace rules differ by place. All of them are settings you fill in, and you need to check your local rules and your lease. This is a record-keeping tool, not legal, tax or financial advice.

Limits: 10 tenants, 12 months per file, 60 other charges, 400 payments. Payments always go to the oldest unpaid charge. No expense tracking, reminders or invoices.
