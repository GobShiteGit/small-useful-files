What should I check on a paperback interior before upload?

Before you upload, confirm that the PDF's trim size and page count match what you chose in the book setup, that the margins are mirrored for left and right pages, and that the previewer shows nothing cut off. Most upload problems come from a mismatch between the file and the setup.

The checklist

  1. Trim size matches. If the book setup says 6 x 9 in, the PDF pages should be 6 x 9 in. Most PDF viewers show page size in the document properties.
  2. Page count matches. Count the pages in the viewer. Do not rely on the file name alone. If you plan to use a name like "100", confirm the viewer shows 100.
  3. Plan for an even count. Pages are printed on both sides of a sheet, so page counts are normally even. Check the platform's rule on this.
  4. Page 1 is a right-hand page. In a printed book, the first page sits on the right when the book is opened. Page 2 is on the back of it, on the left.
  5. Inside margins are mirrored. Look at page 3 and page 4. The wider margin should be on the spine side of each.
  6. Nothing is near the edge. If so, no bleed is fine. If anything touches the edge, you need a bleed layout.
  7. Ink setting matches the file. A black and white interior should be uploaded as black and white. Color ink costs more and is not needed for a black and white file.
  8. Fonts and lines look right. Zoom in on the thin lines and small text. They should be dark enough to read when printed.
  9. Use the previewer. Page through the whole book, not only the first few pages.

A worked example with made-up numbers

You plan a 6 x 9 in book with 110 pages. You open the PDF and find that:

  • Page size shows 6 x 9 in. Good.
  • The viewer says 110 pages. Good, and it is even.
  • Page 3 has a wider left margin and page 4 has a wider right margin. Good.
  • The last page of the file is a lined notes page, not a blank placeholder. Good.

Then you pick 6 x 9 in, 110 pages, black and white ink and no bleed in the setup, upload, and page through the previewer.

Rules you must confirm yourself

Platforms set minimum margins, allowed page counts, and file requirements. These can differ by trim size and can change over time. This guide does not state them as fact. Look at the platform's current help pages for your exact trim size and page count.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing one trim size in the setup and uploading a PDF made for another.
  • Changing the page count in the PDF and forgetting to update the setup.
  • Only looking at the first few pages in the previewer.
  • Forgetting that a cover is a separate file with its own requirements.
